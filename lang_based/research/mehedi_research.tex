\section{Summary}
Software engineer and applied ML researcher. My B.Sc.\ thesis at Rajshahi University of Engineering \& Technology (RUET) built TRACE, which finds the university courses that hold students back by combining prerequisite-graph analysis, Bayesian fail-rate models, and sentiment in student feedback. I then checked its answer with causal inference (DoWhy) on real student records. A paper from it is under review at LAK 2027. I also have 3+ years of industry experience building LLM and RAG features, TypeScript and Python backends, and Docker and CI/CD deployments, currently at Aamira Software Solutions.
